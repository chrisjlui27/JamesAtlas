## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| `()` | `o` | 1 |

The empty round boundary — a circle around the void — is the number one. `o` abbreviates it everywhere.

## Plain

One is a circle drawn around nothing.

## Derivation

| form | rule | note |
|---|---|---|
| `()` | [[N.round]] | the round boundary applied to the void, [[void.page]] |
| `o` | definition | abbreviation |

## Confirms

1 = e⁰. The unit is the *first distinction*: in Laws of Form it is the mark; here it is also a number, and the two readings part ways at the very next node.

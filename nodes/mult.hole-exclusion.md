## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| `<□>` | — | −(−∞) = +∞ ; and `(<□>)` would be 1/0 |

The one form James declares not well-formed: the angle boundary around the black hole. Every other combination of the three empty boundaries is something or nothing; this one is refused.

## Plain

You may not mirror the bottomless pit; that would be dividing by zero.

## Derivation

none — adjoined, as a decree about the form built from [[Z.angle]] and [[mult.black-hole]]. The calculus does not derive the exclusion; it legislates it, because admitting `<□>` and the rules that govern mirrors would let `(<□>) = 1/0` enter the multiplicative layer.

## Confirms

Division by zero as *the* exclusion of arithmetic — and nothing else is excluded. The JH extension found the same decree bans a second thing: inverting a singular matrix (JH-20: a biquaternion with norm form void demands `<□>`). One form-exclusion, two classical exclusions. What the decree does **not** settle is `[[ ]]`, the square of the black hole: James left it undefined without excluding it; the census carries it as an open form.

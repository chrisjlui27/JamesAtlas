## Plain

Adding nothing to a pile leaves the pile.

## Confirms

0 is the additive identity. Note what it is **not**: zero has no inscription, so "0" cannot be pointed at as a form until the black hole gives it one — `(□) ≐ void` at [[mult.hole-instance]] — and it cannot be *negated* as a form until [[Z.void-inverse]]. The number zero has three faces in the chart: nothing, the instance of the hole, and the mirror of nothing.

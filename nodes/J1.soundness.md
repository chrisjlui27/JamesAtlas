## Law

| statement | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| ℂ with ω = 2πi models 𝕁₁: strict rules exact, the unit mod Λ, absorption exact; hence consistent, and ω ≠ void | — | the universal cover of ℂ× is the model; 𝕁₀ is the fibre Λ = 0 |

The consistency of 𝕁₁, by a model: the same ℂ reading the chart has used since [[mult.c-reading]], with one change — the form `[()]` evaluates to 2πi, a generator of the kernel of exp, rather than to the principal 0. In that reading every strict rule is an identity of complex arithmetic, the witnessed unit is an identity mod 2πiℤ whose remainder is the winding of the content, and absorption is exact. So no derivation of 𝕁₁ proves `()` ≡ void, and `[()]` is not void: ω is a genuine new element. The classical calculus is the quotient Λ = 0.

## Plain

Everything 𝕁₁ says is true of complex numbers, once you let the log of 1 be 2πi instead of 0. So 𝕁₁ cannot contradict itself, and ω is really there.

## Confirms

ℂ-soundness of the page-logic draft: 𝕁₁ is the James algebra that is *correct* for complex numbers — the one whose unit says what ln ∘ exp actually does. It is **not** completeness: that every true congruence mod Λ is derivable is a different and open question (the exp–log identity problem). And it is **not** a syntactic consistency proof (confluence and termination of the rewrite system); that is the completion pass the ledger of [[J1.annihilation]] still owes.

## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| `([A]) ≡ A` | the instance of the abstract of A is A, strictly | e^{ln a} = a — on every branch of ln |

Half of James's Involution ([[mult.involution]]), kept whole: the instance of an abstract is the thing itself, with no condition and no receipt. In the ℂ reading this is exp ∘ ln = id, true for every nonzero a whichever log you took, because exp forgets the branch. 𝕁₁ splits Involution here: this half is strict (≡), the other half ([[J1.unit]]) is witnessed (≃).

## Plain

Circle a box and you are back where you started — always. (Boxing a circle is the half that costs something.)

## Derivation

none — adjoined: the first axiom of 𝕁₁, which is the first axiom of the classical calculus read in one direction, stated in terms of the two boundaries [[N.round]] and [[mult.square]]. Its classical shadow is the counit row of [[mult.involution]]; every counit step on the trunk is strict in 𝕁₁ (receipt prover: `census/j1-ledger.md`).

## Confirms

exp ∘ ln = id on ℂ× unconditionally — the asymmetry of the two halves of Involution is the asymmetry of exp and ln: exp is single-valued, ln is not. In the adjunction reading, `( )` ⊣ `[ ]` is a reflective localization and this is the strict counit of a fully faithful right adjoint. It is **not** the unit `[(A)] ≡ A`, which fails by a lattice element: `[(J J)]` is void, J J is ω.

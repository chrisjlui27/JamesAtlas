## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| `([(A)] B) ≃ (A B)` | the unit, taken directly inside an instance, is exact: the instance pays the receipt | e^{(a − kω) + b} = e^{a + b} |

The seam decree of the classical setting ([[mult.seam-decree]]) has two clauses: a unit step is admitted *by position* when it sits directly inside an instance, and *by content* when its content is on the strip. In 𝕁₁ the first clause is this theorem — one witnessed step, absorbed — and the second is [[J1.unit]] itself at depth 0, posting its receipt. Nothing is refused, so nothing has to be detected: the question the gap [[mult.seam-signature]] asks ("which content is on the strip, syntactically?") does not arise for either clause.

## Plain

Undo a box inside a circle and nothing can go wrong, because whatever whole turns you picked up, the circle swallows them. That is the rule the classical chart had to decree and 𝕁₁ simply proves.

## Confirms

The depth-1 criterion: a 2πi error is harmless exactly where an exp will swallow it. On the trunk this is every *by position* guard, now an absorbed receipt — [[C.period-absorption]], [[Q.reciprocal-of-negative]], the seam steps of [[C.de-moivre]] — and the trunk's tapes list them (`census/j1-ledger.md`). What 𝕁₁ does **not** dissolve: a unit step *under a square* before any instance. There ≃ does not pass (a lattice offset inside a log is not a lattice offset of the log), so the step is sound only when its receipt is void, which only the reading can see; the classical *by content* guards under squares survive into 𝕁₁ as imports (`alg.power-of-base`, `alg.sqrt4`, `C.i-to-the-i` and five more). The gap [[mult.seam-signature]] is therefore closed for positions inside instances and at depth 0, and remains open — as a receipt check performed by the reading — under squares.

## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| `<ooo>` | — | −3 |

A negative number is a pile inside the angle boundary. By inverse collection ([[Z.collection]]) it is the same as a pile of mirrored units, `<o><o><o>`: the two ways of writing −3 are equivalent, so "mirror the pile" and "pile the mirrors" agree.

## Plain

Minus three is three circles behind the mirror.

## Derivation

| form | rule | note |
|---|---|---|
| `<ooo>` | [[Z.angle]] | the angle boundary around the pile 3, [[N.numeral]] |
| `−3` | name | the numeral |

## Confirms

ℤ's negative half as inscriptions. The reading "−3 is 0 less 3" is literal here: `<ooo>` is what, added to `ooo`, gives the void. It is **not** "a point to the left of zero" — the calculus has no line and no left; order is a gap ([[N.order]]).

## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| ω/n well formed at level k ⟺ `(ψʳ − 1)(ω/n) ∈ Λ` for all r coprime to n | ψʳ is the cardinality power — r copies, `([A][r])`, winding r times | n | r^{2k} − 1 for all r coprime to n ⟺ λ(n) | 2k |

The receptacle ([[J1.receptacle]]) over-generates: every fraction ω/n is a form and an n-th root of unity, and nothing in the calculus so far says which of them belong to the arithmetic of height one. The wall is the selection rule, and it is native: the operation that acts on a fraction of ω is cardinality — r copies, `([A][r])`, the r-th power map, which winds r times — and at weight 2k it acts by r^{2k}. A fraction ω/n is *well formed at level k* when every cardinality power coprime to n fixes it mod Λ: `n | r^{2k} − 1`. That is a condition the calculus can state about its own forms, using only piles, the square and Λ. Its largest solutions are the numbers of the sphere: M(2) = 24, M(4) = 240, M(6) = 504 ([[J1.wall-census]]).

## Plain

Not every slice of the circle passes the wall: ω/n passes at level k only if winding it r times, for every r sharing no factor with n, brings it back up to whole turns. The slices that pass are counted by 24, 240, 504.

## Derivation

none — adjoined, as a well-formedness decree on the forms `([ω] <[o^{n}]>)` of [[J1.receptacle]], built from the cardinality power ([[mult.cardinality]], [[schema.power]]) and the lattice ([[J1.period-form]]). The content it legislates over is native; what is legislated is the selection: that only the ψ-fixed fractions are *in* the arithmetic.

## Confirms

The image of J: the ψ-wall at level k selects the n with λ(n) | 2k, and the largest of them is the denominator of B_{2k}/4k — the order of im J in π_{4k−1} ([[J1.von-staudt-clausen]], an import). The chart's cardinality power is the Adams operation ψʳ with its K-theory grading read as winding — the point of the July session was that the grading need not be imported. It is **not** yet im J as a *module* (products like η³ = 12ν as forms need Ext¹ data — O1 of the July set), and it is **not** a theorem: it is a rule about which forms count, and it carries the price of a decree. Which weights 2k are realized (the 4k − 1 pattern) is also still imported.

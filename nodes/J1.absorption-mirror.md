## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| `(<ω> A) ≃ (A)` | a mirrored period inside an instance is absorbed too | e^{a − 2πi} = e^a |

Absorption for the negative half of the lattice: with [[J1.absorption]] for ω and this for `<ω>`, every λ ∈ Λ = ℤω is absorbed by an instance, k steps at a time.

## Plain

A turn the other way is still a whole turn; inside a circle it changes nothing either.

## Derivation

| form | rule | note |
|---|---|---|
| `(<ω> A)` | given | a mirrored period beside A, inside an instance |
| `(ω <ω> A)` | [[J1.absorption]] | right to left: a period may be *inserted* inside an instance as freely as removed — the receipt is paid either way |
| `(A)` | [[Z.inversion]] | `ω <ω>` cancels: strict |

## Confirms

e^{a − 2πi} = e^a. It is **not** a second axiom: it is absorption and Inversion. The receipt of row 2 is −ω by sign convention (a period inserted), paid by the instance; the prover grades it absorbed.

## Law

| statement | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| receipts are a linear context: exchange yes; contraction no (merging receipts collapses Λ = ℤω to its torsion and destroys the wall); weakening no (discarding receipts is absorption everywhere, the classical floor) | — | 𝕁₁ : classical calculus :: linear logic : classical logic |

The one discipline of 𝕁₁, and it is about the tape, not the page. A receipt may be moved past other receipts (exchange) and cancelled against its mirror (the loop ω ω̄ ⇒ void); it may not be copied, and it may not be thrown away. Weakening on receipts — discarding — is the old A5 absorption applied everywhere, which returns the classical calculus; contraction — merging multiplicities — collapses the grading: in ℤω, 2ω ~ ω forces ω ~ 0, and with the lattice go ω/24 and the ψ-wall.

## Plain

Every turn you take gets one ticket, and the ticket can be handed round but not torn up or photocopied. Tear them all up and you are back in James's calculus; photocopy them and the arithmetic of the wall is gone.

## Confirms

The polarized-linear-logic dictionary of 𝕁 v3.1: `( )` and collection as positive and negative connectives, the receipt tape as a Geometry-of-Interaction execution trace, L0 as the linearity of the context, and 𝕁₀ = 𝕁₁ + (W, C) as classical = linear + structural rules. It is **not** a rule of the page: no form is affected; it is a rule about proofs. And it is **not** yet enforced for *exchange* in any interesting sense by the engine, whose tape is a multiset (exchange is free); what the engine enforces is the absence of weakening and of contraction within a derivation, as the row above says.

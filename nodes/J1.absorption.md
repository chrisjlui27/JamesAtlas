## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| `(ω A) ≃ (A)` | a period inside an instance is absorbed — and the receipt ω is written on the tape, paid by the instance | e^{a + 2πi} = e^a |

The July set's A5′, "an instance absorbs any interior period, exp kills the whole kernel", is in 𝕁₁ a single application of the witnessed unit: `[()]` → void, taken *directly inside an instance*. The instance pays the receipt on the spot, so the two sides are exactly equal in value; the receipt is nonetheless written, which is what makes 𝕁₁ *look like* the classical calculus inside instances while keeping the tape outside them (LEGISLATION 30a). For λ = kω apply it k times; for the mirror see [[J1.absorption-mirror]].

## Plain

Inside a circle a whole turn changes nothing — e^{a + 2πi} = e^a — and 𝕁₁ writes down that it swallowed a turn.

## Derivation

| form | rule | note |
|---|---|---|
| `(ω A)` | given | a period ([[J1.period-form]]) beside A, inside an instance |
| `([()] A)` | definition | ω unfolded |
| `(A)` | [[J1.unit]] | the unit at void content, `[()] ≃ void`, directly inside the instance — **absorbed**: the receipt ω is paid by the instance (receipt prover: absorbed, k = 1) |

## Confirms

e^{z + 2πi} = e^z: the kernel of exp is Λ, all of it. On the chart this is the law behind every "`[o] ≐ void` inside an instance" of the trunk — [[mult.log-of-one]] used in an absorbing position — and behind [[C.period-absorption]] `(A J J) ≐ (A)`. In the July presentation A5′ had to be *forced*: with a unit weak only mod Λ and no receipts, the critical pair `([()][A])` reduces to A by one branch and to `(ω [A])` by the other, and they join only if absorption holds. With receipts the pair is not critical — both branches are ≃-equal with tapes that cancel — and the law is a theorem. It is **not** strict: the tape is not empty, it is paid. Evaluation (≈, [[J1.classical-floor]]) forgets the payment; 𝕁₁ does not.

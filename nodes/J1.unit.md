## Law

| form | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| `[(A)] ≃ A` | the abstract of the instance of A is A *up to a period*, and the period is written down | [e^a] = a − kω with k = ⌈(Im a − π)/2π⌉ for e^a ≠ 1, the number of whole turns beyond the strip; the receipt is what the step forgets, [e^a] − a = −kω — and on the lattice, a = mω, where e^a is exactly one and the log reads 2πi, the receipt is (1 − m)ω: ω at a = 0, void at a = ω |

The other half of Involution, and the one axiom-half 𝕁₁ changes. The abstract of an instance returns the content *mod Λ*: the two sides differ by a lattice element, the **receipt**, which the step posts on a tape rather than discarding. In the ℂ reading the receipt is the lattice element the step forgets — the reading's log of e^a minus a: void on the principal strip −π < Im a ≤ π, −ω, −2ω… above it and ω, 2ω… below, and on the lattice itself, a = mω, where e^a is exactly one and the log reads 2πi, (1 − m)ω — ω at a = 0, void at a = ω (LEGISLATION 30b as amended; witness `tools/models/j1_soundness.py`). Nothing is refused — where the classical calculus needed a decree to keep `J J ≐ void` from being proved ([[mult.seam-decree]]), 𝕁₁ lets `J J ≃ void` be proved and records that the proof cost ω.

## Plain

Box a circle and you get back what you circled — except that going round the circle may have added a whole turn, and 𝕁₁ writes that turn on a receipt instead of pretending it did not happen.

## Derivation

none — adjoined, in terms of the two boundaries [[N.round]] and [[mult.square]]: the unit half of `mult.involution` (see also), weakened. The receipt is not a form on the page: it is the name of the identification `[(A)] ≃ A`, a 2-cell in the two-cell presentation, and its value — a multiple of the period form [[J1.period-form]] — is read off by the reading. The engine's position rule for the receipt (SPEC §7.3): **absorbed** when the step is taken directly inside an instance (through angles at most) — the instance pays it, and the result is value-exact; **posted** at depth 0 — the two forms agree mod Λ; under a square the step is not taken at all, because ≃ does not pass through `[ ]` (the depth-one criterion, `J1.seam-theorem`).

## Confirms

ln ∘ exp = id mod 2πiℤ — the multivaluedness of the logarithm stated as an equation with a remainder rather than as a prohibition. James's two constraints of procedure (p. 47), the chart's seam decree, and this axiom are three responses to the same fact; this is the one that keeps the information. It is **not** a weakening of the calculus's power: every classical law still holds, strictly or with absorbed receipts or mod Λ ([[J1.classical-floor]]). It is **not** a statement about which branch is principal: the receipt names the difference between two logs, never a preferred one — the design rule "no absolute laws, only differences and exchanges" (𝕁 v3.1).

## Notes

Together with [[J1.counit]] this axiom replaces [[mult.involution]] in the price of every 𝕁₁ law (the generator substitutes the split pair, and the unit only where the tape has a receipt — SPEC §4.3); the counit is base, this is the first extra. [[J1.linearity]] says what may be done with a receipt: exchanged, never duplicated, never dropped.

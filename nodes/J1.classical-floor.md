## Law

| statement | units | standard |
|---|---|---|
| forget the tape and 𝕁₁ is James's calculus; keep it and every classical law is still there, graded | — | classical = linear + structural rules |

The theorem that makes 𝕁₁ an *enrichment* rather than a replacement. Define evaluation ≈ as ≃ with weakening and contraction adjoined on receipts — the tape discarded — and the classical calculus is recovered: that is T-L1 of 𝕁 v3.1. The receipt prover makes the claim concrete law by law — for the 93 derivations the prover grades (the trunk's theorems and schemas; its gaps, decrees, imports and definitions have no derivation to grade, and `C.phase-unique`, a meta theorem, fails in 𝕁₁ for its own stated reason): 77 are strict or have only absorbed receipts (𝕁₁ agrees to the letter), 7 hold mod Λ (a receipt posted at depth 0), 8 hold under a guard (a unit step under a square, sound by the reading's receipt check at the generic points of the domain), and exactly one fails at generic points — [[alg.one-to-power]], 1^B = 1, replaced by the receptacle ([[J1.receptacle]]) because the value 1 has a lattice of logs. The receipt check has *exceptional points*: where a content is exactly one the square reads ω, and five of the eight guarded laws (`alg.power-of-power`, `alg.root-of-product`, `alg.root-of-quotient`, `C.root-of-negative`, `C.modulus-of-polar`) have a non-void receipt there — at A = B = 1, √(AB) = √A √B says √1 = (√1)², and in the reading √1 is `(([ω] <[oo]>))`, the instance of ω/2, which is −1: the receptacle at n = 2. The tapes name the points; the laws stay `guarded`, which is what the guard now means: sound at generic points, false in 𝕁₁'s reading on the lattice.

## Plain

Tear up every receipt and you are back in James's calculus; keep them and every old law is still true, at a stated price. The one law that does not survive said that 1 to any power is 1.

## Confirms

"The thesis stands to this calculus exactly as classical logic stands to linear logic" (𝕁 v3.1) — now a counted statement. It is **not** a proof that *every* classical derivation has an empty tape (77 of 93 do, counting absorbed as paid); the seven posted and eight guarded are the honest residue, and the one failure is a gain. And it is **not** the statement that the guarded laws are true in 𝕁₁: five of them are false at the exceptional points of the reading, exactly as 1^{1/n} = 1 is — the same fact, met under a square.

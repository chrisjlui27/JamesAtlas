The "half of O^×" estimate of the units without a log was also wrong: the index-2 coset is the first filter and the commuting requirement refuses almost all of the rest (≈ 96 % of 2000 sampled units). *Repaired:* `H.ramified-place` (law, units, derivation row, Notes), `H.hurwitz-units` (Confirms, JP cell), SPEC §6.4, LEGISLATION 32(e), ROADMAP §7, README say the 24 are the torsion of the *global* order and the torsion the reading names; the witness checks (3i + 4j)/5 and samples 2000 units.
2. **"Fifteen trunk laws fail on generic quaternions and hold on the slice" was untested by the ledger and false for 8 of the 15 on the `slice` domain.** The ledger grades on the plane; the forms pin J and ω to i, so a trunk law that mentions them fails on a random slice. *Repaired:* "on the plane ℂ ⊂ ℍ" everywhere, and SPEC §1 (slice) and §6.4 say the Slice Principle's J → J_u is a substitution the forms do not make.
3. **A false law was certified `holds` when no sample could be judged** — `check_equal_forms` skipped every overflowing sample and returned true, `_values_agree` and the premise checks were vacuously true. *Repaired:* a law no sample can judge fails; a value check nothing could compare is false; a premise no sample could test is not established (SPEC §2.4, §7.1).

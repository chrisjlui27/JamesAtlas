## 1. The three target pictures, in the chart's terms

The chart already has the shape all three targets need; what is missing is the rungs that make the shape *native*.

| target | what it is, in the chart | what is missing |
|---|---|---|
| **motivic** | a node is a form; its `readings` row (ℂ, finset, λ, CC, logic …) is its row of *realizations*; a law that holds in every reading is the stable part. The form is the motive, the readings are its realizations, and the "comparison boundary B between pages" (motivic thread, 2026-10-03) is the comparison isomorphism between realizations | the readings are columns filled by hand; nothing on the page *is* a reading. The rung that makes a reading a form is `struct` (a page as a form) and `logic` (the internal language in which "for every reading" can be said) |
| **F₁ (finset / BPQ road)** | `( )` and `[ ]` as finite sets, `< >` as group completion; the additive fragment `void`–`N` is EXACT in finset today, and the inverse boundary is where the sphere spectrum enters | the `finset` cells are mostly `ABSENT`/`untested` past `N`; J's finset analogy (η) matches the *J1* J (2J = ω), not the classical one — so the F₁ road passes through `J1`, not around it |
| **sheaf of formal groups over the (classifying topos of the) Berkovich space** | the Berkovich spectrum of ℤ is the space of places; the chart's `JP` setting is "the same wall `(ω A) ≐ (A)` at every place", with the place a pile declared void. The wall at a place is a formal group of height one (Λ = the period lattice of exp = Ĝ_m at ∞; the ψ-wall selecting ω/n is its torsion, the im-J arithmetic). The *stack* of lattices — level 2, "a syntax fibered over the moduli stack of its own lattices" — is the moduli of formal groups with its height stratification. The sheaf is the chart itself once the base (places) and the fibre (the lattice per place) are rungs rather than columns | a `place` rung before the branches; a `J1` rung that makes Λ native (closing `mult.seam-signature`); a `J2`/moduli rung that does not yet exist in `RUNGS`; and the schema machinery (induction) without which μ_n for all n — the torsion of the formal group — cannot be stated |

So: **yes, the target adjusts the order**, in three ways. The places move before the division algebras; the moduli rung is inserted; and the induction gap stops being a gap left for later and becomes the first thing to build, because every one of the three pictures needs schemas over n.

- **`J1` before everything else.** It is where the chart's biggest import is paid off: the seam decree is stated in the ℂ reading because no syntactic signature of "off the strip" exists (`mult.seam-signature`). ## 6. `place` — as built (2026-10-06; LEGISLATION 31, pending Lui)

Lui's instruction was "continue the spec through rung place"; the rung was built on the session's recommendations, which are recorded as LEGISLATION 31 for his confirmation. What was decided, and why:

1. **The page is a pile declared void, and that is the whole datum.** The JP ledger (2026-09-13) found that the three place-data — which fractions of the period are forms, the irreducible `[p]`, the radius of the instance — are one datum, the void pile. The rung is built that way: `place.void-pile` first, everything else downstream. The lens of JP v0.2 is *not* drawn: the depth is a parameter of the page, and the completion is the gap. Reversing this means a fourth boundary on the chart before `H`'s ordered bracket, which the 2026-09-19 note suggested might be the same notation (`[x ; y]`) — the reason to wait.
2. **The reading is 𝕁₁'s, at p.** The log of exactly one reads the place's period, as LEGISLATION 30(b) says it does at ∞; so `J = ω_p/2`, `J J ≃ ω_p`, and the wall is literally the same law. The alternative — the classical page at p, with `[()]` void and the torsion read mod p − 1 — would make the place rung a classical rung; the chart's trunk is already graded in 𝕁₁, so the places are read in 𝕁₁ too. Three conventions had to be chosen with no analogue at ∞: the *generator* of the torsion (the Teichmüller lift of the least primitive root, −1 at 2 — the referee found the earlier draft silent on it, and that √4 reads −2 or 2 at 5, 7, 13 according to the generator), the *principal root* (a fraction of a torsion count in 0 … p−2 — canonical on a principal unit, where it is the principal unit with that power: √4 = −2 at 3, a fact; elsewhere a choice the generator makes) and *weight 2* (the product of two formal elements has no value at a finite place — the JP v0.3 finding that weight is visible at finite places exactly because periods do not multiply to numbers there).
3. **The trunk's JP cells are computed.** The earlier hand cells came from the jadic.js audit; the engine's reading replaces them. The surprises: e is not p-adic (`alg.base` and its kin are `replaced`, not `holds`); the log laws post at p exactly what they post at ∞ — by position, at depth 0 — and hold mod Λ_p for the same reason they hold mod Λ there (the first draft said "the torsion receipt"; the referee showed the tapes are identical); `alg.sqrt4` fails at p = 3 under the principal root; the rung-C turn laws hold at every place *for integer turns* only, and are `guarded` (a turn whose count is a fraction of principal units is sampled too, since the second draft); the "for units" guards of rung `Q` were too weak (ω/3 is not a form at 5 though 3 is a unit) and are now the selection rule; the grades that change from ∞ to the places are imports whose content is formless at p, which become absent there.
4. **"At every place" is priced by an import.** Ostrowski's theorem names the places; the chart cannot prove it before the metric rung. Every law over p cites it as a schema over n cites the induction gap — and it names the places without licensing the passage from the five the engine reads at to all of them, as the induction gap does not license the passage from a range to all n.
5. **Fermat–Euler is imported.** `place.fermat` (U^{φ(p^n)} ≃ 1) has no derivation on the page — the counting argument (Lagrange) needs an order or a count of forms the chart does not have (`N.order`) — so it is an `import`, checked by the engine at every instance of its range and priced into `place.division-by-unit` and `place.lift`, which rest on it. (The first draft called it "one law on the engine's word" and typed it a schema with a statement table; the referee found three such nodes — Fermat, the void pile, the radius — and each is now what it is: an import, a decree, a theorem of the reading with its witness.) The rung's first owed derivation, ahead of the completion gap.
6. **The discipline of the page** (found by the referee, 2026-10-06). The first draft let the void pile act as a rewrite rule anywhere and stated `[p]^{n} ≐ □` (n copies of `[p]` are the pole at depth n); together they prove `o^{5} ≐ void` at p = 5, depth 2, and `oo ≐ o` at depth 1 — the page collapses. The repair: the page's equality (mod p^n) is a congruence through juxtaposition, angles and instances but not through a square, so the laws of the page are ≃ and the receipt prover grades a page step by position — `page` with no square anywhere above it, and otherwise *measured at every square above it*: an import where no square's value changes, absorbed or posted where some change by a period (the outermost changed square posts the receipt, the instance directly outside it pays it), absent otherwise (SPEC §7.3). The second referee pass found the first draft of that rule stopping at the first instance above the step — an instance only page-equalizes a page difference, and a square above it still reads the period at the exactly-one point — and two precision-inflation paths in the engine's equality; all three are closed (the working precision is now the depth plus a margin, and a value that has lost more shells cannot be judged). The pole is withdrawn as a finite count: n·[p] is a finite log whose *instance* is the void pile (`place.prime-power`), and the pole is the limit end (`place.two-ends`). The same measurement would be sound for a unit step under a square at ∞; it is not applied to the trunk, and is offered as a proposal.

**What the rung does not do.** The adelic page (one re-entry on every place's circle; ∞'s share, the realized weights) is `J2`'s; the metric (|·|_p, the product formula, Berkovich) is `analysis`'s; the Hurwitz places (where the quaternions ramify) are `H`'s, which is next (LEGISLATION 29). The JH and JO cells of every place node are `untested` for that reason.

**Next: `H`.** Read against the places while they are fresh: the ordered bracket, the seed J_j, the first non-commutative wall — and the places where ℍ over ℚ ramifies (2 and ∞ for Hamilton's quaternions), which is where the two rungs meet.

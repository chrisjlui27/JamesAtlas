# Legislation

The binding design decisions for the Boundary Chart, numbered as in the design questions of 2026-10-03. "Default" means Lui accepted the proposed default; his own words are quoted where he gave them. A later session changes a decision only by adding a dated amendment below it, never by editing the line.

**0. Continuity.** This chart framing supersedes the 2026-10-02 "game" framing. The game becomes a later skin over the same files (§12 of SPEC).

**1. Start.** At the void; the natural numbers are the first rung. A pile cannot be stated without void and `o`.

**2. The F₁ end.** One target node with three roads in: (a) finite sets / sphere spectrum as the trunk; (b) Borger λ-rings and (c) Connes–Consani as planned roads. Lui: "Uncertain. Default as the path with an eye to expand the expressibility of the other ways." Implementation: (b) and (c) are readings on every trunk node before they are nodes; no trunk node is settled with blank (b)/(c) cells.

**3. Loop.** Yes: a DAG with exactly one marked re-entry edge, `F1.arithmetic → void.page`, carrying the chart's largest gap.

**4. Node types.** Primitive, Axiom, Definition, Theorem, Decree, Import, Gap — all seven are steps; type is colour and price. Most steps will be theorems.

**5. Granularity.** One law per node. Concepts are a second index pointing at the laws that express them.

**6. Back-links.** Mechanical: exactly the rules cited in the Given–Rule derivation; checkable by the engine. Curated relevance is a separate `see_also` field.

**7. Display.** Raw forms beside named units beside standard notation; the equality in force is recorded on every node.

**8. Settings.** An attribute (a per-setting `holds` table), not a branch. An extension is itself a node; a setting is a named set of nodes.

**9. Bricken.** James 1993 is the ground truth for the classical setting. Bricken's constructs enter as attributed notes tagged by volume, and as nodes only where they add a law.

**10. Notation.** Canonical strings in `( ) [ ] < >` for machines; rendering shows `o` / `□` / mirror or drawn boundaries.

**11. Price.** Computed from the links, never hand-entered: the non-derivable ancestors (primitives, axioms, decrees, imports), split into the base three-and-three and the extras.

**12. Status.** One enum: NATIVE, NATIVE-BY-DECREE, IMPORTED, ANALOGY, CONJECTURE, REFUTED, ABSENT. Separate verification tier (none / hand / engine / numeric / lean). Separate `known_since` (the mathematics) and `entered` (the program).

**13. Two kinds of gap.** Concept-without-form and form-without-reading. A mechanical census of all small forms is part of the chart.

**14. Gap plans.** Fields: candidate form; law(s) to add and their type; nativity criterion; refutation test; owner thread. A new primitive is allowed but ranks last.

**15. Refuted readings.** Kept, struck through, attached to the law they were mistaken for; collected into one searchable list.

**16. Scope for v1.** A (number and algebra) and B (analysis) in full; D (structure) as far as the F₁ road needs; C (logic) as one strand; E (The Field) as pointers.

**17. Induction.** A named gap, the first the reader meets. Lui: "Meta-moves may wind up being necessary. The moduli-stack is a sort of meta-primitive idea that decomposes into 'readings' in the calculus is my sort of understanding. Start with the named gap." Implementation: a `level: meta` on nodes; `readings` as a first-class table; gap plans carry a `meta-move?` field.

**18. Order.** Dependency order is the spine; rungs (number systems) are the chapters; every node carries a shape-led / need-led tag.

**19. Trunk.** The 𝕁₁ road is the trunk; the adelic page is the second road into F₁; the division-algebra branch rejoins at 24, marked ANALOGY.

**20. Verification.** Lui: "I was originally just wanting to remove the Lean portion. I think we can have (c) as the core option, with (b) as a later scoped expansion to make everything legible in that setting." So: the rewrite engine, fixture corpus, `jadic.js` and the receipt prover carry verification now; Lean is a later scoped expansion over the classical trunk, with node ids chosen to become declaration names without renaming.

**21. Process.** Propose → refute → repair with an independent referee, per rung; nodes written to be picked up cold; "Exact" withheld until engine-verified.

**22. Source of truth.** One Markdown file per node in a repository. Lui: "if I have to set up something for this to be workable, let's set it up." A git repository (`boundary-chart`) with a checker and a generator; Obsidian-compatible links.

**23. Visual.** v1: forms drawn from canonical strings, and the chart as a zoomable DAG. v2: a live instrument per law, scoped node by node. The game, if it survives, is a skin.

**24. Audience.** Lui: "Mainly me. Essentially, the goal is to see how much of this exists AND to use it to walk the line and educate myself on the concepts I don't understand yet in the language I have already in my command, the earlier nodes confirming this understanding for me in house. Secondly, if I can use it to teach myself to understand, then I could use it to teach someone else to understand." Implementation: a required one-sentence Plain statement and a Confirms section on every node; a personal `understood` tracker rendered as walk progress; K–12 band visible.

**25. Seeding.** A fresh uniform walk for the trunk to ℂ, then backfill of the branches from the existing ledgers with their ids kept as aliases; the graded K–12 band visible on every node.

**26. Pilot.** Spec plus a pilot of the first rungs so the schema meets real forms before the trunk is walked. The pilot delivered 2026-10-03 covers `void`, `N`, `Z`, and the opening of `mult`.

**Amendment A1 (2026-10-03, proposed by the session, pending Lui's confirmation).** The classical setting carries two decrees from rung `mult` on: the undefined forms (`<□>` and `[□]` excluded, up to ≐) and the seam discipline (a unit step `[(X)] ≐ X`, in either orientation, only on content inside the principal strip or in an absorbing position). Both were forced by referee passes that collapsed the unrestricted theory with laws already on the chart. The seam discipline is stated in the ℂ reading — an import inside the classical setting — because no syntactic signature of off-strip content exists (`mult.seam-signature`). If Lui prefers James's own formulation (name J first, then state the J-discipline at rung C), the decree node moves to rung C and the sign laws that cross the seam move with it.

**Amendment A2 (2026-10-03, proposed by the session after the rung-C referee pass, pending Lui's confirmation).** `NATIVE-BY-DECREE` is reserved for statements that hold because a decree was issued (exclusions, the decrees, the meta theorems about the decree-governed calculus). A node whose derivation passes through a unit step that the seam decree adjudicates stays `NATIVE` and declares `guarded_by: [mult.seam-decree]`, which the checker computes from the derivation: *by content* when the step is at a non-absorbing position (the ℂ reading certifies the content is on the strip), *by position* when the step is absorbing but its content leaves the strip for some value of the domain (a seam step). An absorbing on-strip step is free, and so is a unit step on void or hole content (`[(void)] ≐ void`, `[(□)] ≐ □` are theorems of the hole rules, not instances of the decree). Price inherits guards through `uses`, so descendants are not re-tagged. Reason: the previous convention priced identical steps differently in rungs Q and C (the referee's BLOCKER), left absorbing seam steps unpriced, and called exact identities of ℂ "by decree". Reversing this amendment means restoring `NATIVE-BY-DECREE` on `Q.reciprocal-of-negative`, `Q.negative-denominator`, `Q.negatives-cancel` and the four rung-C laws now guarded by position, and narrowing `check_guarded_by` to the content clause.

**Note (2026-10-03).** The thesis — J. M. James, *A Calculus of Number Based on Spatial Forms*, M.S. thesis, University of Washington, 1993 — is in `references/` and is the ground truth for the `classical` setting. Its chapter plan is: 3 Boundary Mathematics; 4 Instance and Abstract (two-boundary calculus, N, cardinality, dominion); 5 Inverse (three-boundary calculus, Z, Q, division by zero, negative and fractional cardinality); 6 Phase (J, phase independence, J cancellation, multiple value and the two constraints, exp/log, e, π, trigonometry); 7 Future work (incl. boundary differentiation, Table 7.1); Appendix A conversion and formulas; Appendix B examples (base multiplication with carry, √9, fractions, (a+b)(a−b), the quadratic formula). Earlier citations to "Chapter 4/5" for the phase material were wrong and have been corrected to Chapter 6 with page numbers.

**30. The shape of `J1` (2026-10-04).** Decided on ROADMAP §5's six questions. (a) **Total space, not values** — Lui: "Let's go with T. It LOOKS like P in some settings but has the tools to be 'unbaked' naturally." The calculus proper has no absorption: the strict rules are the counit `([A]) ≡ A`, Distribution, Inversion and the medium; the unit `[(A)] ≃ A` is witnessed by a receipt; the period `(ω) ≃ ()` and absorption `(λ A) ≃ (A)`, λ ∈ Λ = ℤω, are witnessed moves inside an instance (what makes J1 "look like P" there); evaluation ≈ is *defined* as adjoining weakening and contraction on the receipt context (v3.1, T-L1), and the classical numbers are that functor's image. Three equalities, as SPEC §1 already carries them: ≡ strict convertibility, ≃ witnessed, ≈ evaluated. (b) **ω evaluates to 2πi** in the ℂ reading of `setting: J1` nodes — the form `[()]`, and only that form, is the chosen lift of the generator; ≃ is congruence mod Λ at log level (accepted as stated). (c) **The receipt prover is built now**, not staged — Lui: "Prover now." It is a *checker*, not a searcher: each derivation row must be obtained from the previous one by the cited rule, applied once (or the stated number of times) at some position, with the strict rules leaving the tape untouched and the unit, the period and absorption posting receipts; a law is ≡ iff a derivation exists with an empty tape, ≃ iff one exists with a tape, and the ℂ reading names each receipt's value. (d) **Stratified annihilation is to be decided within the program** — Lui: "This can be decided within the context of the program. My guess is that moving from J0 to J1 is the shift from inversion to void and inversion to period, and period gets absorbed within an instance with a receipt." The chart records the guess as a conjecture node and lets the prover's completion pass decide the strata, as the multiplicative identity decided A5′. (e) **The ψ-wall belongs to `J1`** — Lui: "Agreed, the wall is well-defined now and belongs at height 1." (f) **□ is the pole, polarity-dependent** — Lui: "Okay, that aligns with my thoughts. Let's go with B." Inside an instance the empty square is ✠ (concession: `(A □) ≡ void`, strict); outside it is Ω, the Λ-indeterminate log of nothing ("pole = Λ"), so dominion `□ λ ≡ □` forgets nothing determinate and L0 needs no exemption; a receipt that reaches the pole is an opaque token that never cancels (the daimon's grade, ruled indeterminate 2026-08-23). The fork `[A<A>]` = □ (annihilation to void) or `[ω]` (annihilation to period) is where J1-d and J1-f meet, and the prover's completion pass decides it.

*Amendments to 30 (2026-10-06, proposed by the session after the J1 referee pass, pending Lui's confirmation — as A1 and A2 were).* **(b)** The literal reading — the *form* `[()]` evaluates to 2πi — is not a model: a square's value then depends on more than the value inside it, and `[(o <o>)] → [()]` (Inversion, strict) jumps from 0 to 2πi. Amended to the compositional reading: the log of *exactly one* is the chosen lift ω = 2πi, every other log is principal (SPEC §5.5). Under it the strict rules are exact in every context, `[(J J)]` reads 2πi so the crack's step `[(J J)] → J J` has receipt 0, and the non-void receipt sits at the log of one, `[()] → void` — `mult.log-of-one`, the law 𝕁₁ weakens. Proposed to Lui with the alternative (classical values, receipts as tape tokens only); implemented as the proposal pending his word. **(f)** Refined: a unit step on pole-valued content posts no receipt, because the pole has no winding to record — from inside an instance it is ✠, from outside it is already the whole coset −∞ + Λ. So Dominion `□ A ≡ □` is strict at every depth and its J1 cell is `holds`; "an opaque token that never cancels" describes the daimon of the page logic (the ruling of 2026-08-23), not an entry on the arithmetic's tape. **(d)** The guess is recorded as the leading candidate of a *gap* node (`J1.annihilation`), not a conjecture node: a conjecture needs a law the calculus can state, and the strata are not yet one. **(a)** A theorem whose receipts were all absorbed is reused as a witnessed move, so a law that uses it is ≃: absorption is never promoted to ≡ by reuse. **(c)** The prover's search for the cheapest tape is heuristic; a path it misses can make a grade worse, never better (SPEC §5.5). *Found by the verification pass, for the decision on (b):* under the compositional reading the receipt check that keeps the trunk's eight under-square guards sound has exceptional points — a content exactly one reads ω — and five of the eight (`alg.power-of-power`, `alg.root-of-product`, `alg.root-of-quotient`, `C.root-of-negative`, `C.modulus-of-polar`) have a non-void receipt there: √1 = −1 in the reading, the receptacle at n = 2. **31. The shape of `place` (2026-10-06, proposed by the session on Lui's "continue the spec through rung place", pending his confirmation — as A1, A2 and the amendments to 30).** Eight decisions, each implemented as the proposal; (g) and (h) were forced by the rung's referee pass the same day.

(a) **A place is a pile declared void.** The rung opens with the import [[place.ostrowski]] (the places of ℚ are the primes and ∞) and the decree [[place.void-pile]], `o^{p**n} ≃ void`, written over the place and the depth and checked by the engine at p = 2, 3, 5, 7, 13 and depths 1 … 3: the page at p to depth n is ℤ/p^n. Everything else on the rung — the irreducible `[p]`, the radius, the selection rule — is downstream of it through the reading that reads the page (the import of (b); the JP ledger's "three place-data are one datum"). A decree is priced, so the void pile enters the price of every law of the rung. The depth is a parameter of the page, not a boundary on it; the lens and the completion (ℤ_p as the tower of pages) are the rung's gap, [[place.completion]], with the JP ledger's lens as the candidate.

(b) **The reading at p is the 𝕁₁ reading moved to a finite place** ([[place.reading]], an import like [[mult.c-reading]]): the log space ℤ_p ⊕ ℚ[p] ⊕ ℚJ_p, `[ ]` splitting a number into its three counts and `( )` reassembling them. Five conventions: the log of exactly one reads the place's period ω_p = (p−1)·J_p (LEGISLATION 30b at p — so J = ω_p/2 and the wall is the same wall; "exactly one" is decided at the engine's working precision, above the page's depth); a fraction of a torsion count is taken in 0 … p−2 (0, 1 at 2) — the *principal root* of the place, canonical on a principal unit (at p = 3, √4 = −2: 4 is a principal unit there and −2 the principal unit whose square it is) and a choice of the generator elsewhere (h); a formal element's log is a formal symbol that a count scales, and the product of two formal elements has no value at a finite place (weight 2: `([J][J])`); counts scale formal elements only when exact; the generator of the torsion is (h). SPEC §4.4.

(c) **The price of "at every place" is Ostrowski.** Every law over the place p — schema, decree or import — cites [[place.ostrowski]] in `uses`, as a schema over n cites [[N.induction]] (SPEC §2.7); resting on another schema does not stand in for it. The import names the places and does not license the passage from the five tested primes to all of them: that passage is the import's price, as the passage to all n is the induction gap's. The one theorem of arithmetic the rung borrows whole is Fermat–Euler, [[place.fermat]], an `import` checked by the engine at every instance of its range, whose counting argument the chart cannot yet make; it is priced into [[place.division-by-unit]] and [[place.lift]]. (The first draft typed it, the void pile and the radius as schemas with statement tables — `verified: engine` with nothing derived; the referee's objection stands and each is now an import, a decree, a meta theorem with its witness.)

(d) **The trunk's JP cells are computed**, as the J1 cells are: every law read at the five places on the domain dictionary of SPEC §4.4 (positive = principal units and fractions of them), every licensed derivation re-graded with the place's receipts, the cell compared with the hand cell. Fifty cells were rewritten in all, twenty-four of them `untested`: e is not p-adic ([[alg.base]] and its kin `replaced` by [[place.radius]]); the log of one and the log laws hold mod Λ_p, posted at depth 0 exactly as at ∞ (the first draft said "the torsion receipt"; the referee showed the tapes are the same by position, and that the only grades that change from ∞ to the places are imports whose content is formless at p — the base e, a root the place refuses — which become absent there); [[alg.sqrt4]] `fails` at p = 3 under the principal root; the rung-C turn laws hold for integer turns only and are `guarded` (eleven cells, after fractions of principal units were sampled); i only at p ≡ 1 mod 4. The radius is the standing proviso of every instance (a sample outside it is retried on it and, if still outside, is formless there), and the lattice points are probed by domain and named, as at ∞.

(e) **The same wall at every place is a theorem with a witness** ([[place.wall]]): `(ω A) ≃ (A)` with ω = `[()]` the period of the place, the same absorbed step, and the J1 rung's tapes re-earned at p = 2, 3, 5, 7, 13 by the same receipt prover. The place-specific laws are what differs — residues, minus one as a pile, no reciprocal of the prime, division by a unit as a power, Fermat–Euler, the Teichmüller element as the instance of ω/(p−1), the selection rule, the doll and the geometric doll (JP-1 as a theorem at a depth), the pole at a finite count of `[p]`, the two ends, Hensel's lift, and the local wall e_p(k) whose product is M(2k).

(f) **The JH, JO cells of the rung are `untested` and owed to `H`**, read against the places (LEGISLATION 29). The adelic page — one re-entry counted on every place's circle, with ∞'s share — is owed to `J2` (ROADMAP §4); [[place.local-wall]] factors an integer place by place and is not it.

(g) **The discipline of the page** (found by the referee, 2026-10-06; the blocker of the pass). The page's equality — agreement mod p^n — passes through juxtaposition, angles and instances and **not through a square**: `[o^{p**n}]` is n copies of `[p]`, `[void]` is the pole, and the log of a non-unit loses as many shells as its count of `[p]`. With the void pile a rewrite rule anywhere, and the first draft's `[p]^{n} ≐ □`, the calculus proved `o^{5} ≐ void` at p = 5, depth 2 and `oo ≐ o` at depth 1: the page collapsed. Decided: (i) the laws of the page — the void pile and what follows from it by the page's equality alone ([[place.residue]], [[place.negation-is-a-pile]], [[place.fermat]], [[place.division-by-unit]], [[place.prime-power]], [[place.geometric-doll]]) — are ≃, never ≡; (ii) the receipt prover grades a page step by position: `page` (a grade between strict and absorbed; cell `holds`) with no square anywhere above it — an instance only page-equalizes a page difference, it does not pay it as it pays a period, so a square above an instance still reads the difference at the exactly-one point (the second referee pass, 2026-10-06, which found the first draft of this clause stopping at the first instance) — and with a square above it *measured at every square above it*: an import where no square's value changes at the generic points, absorbed or posted where some change by a period (the outermost changed square posts the receipt, and the instance directly outside it pays it — an import instead when a further square lies above that instance, which reads the page-equal values as equal at the generic points only — or it stays on the tape at depth 0), absent otherwise; a theorem or lemma whose tape carries a page step is a page law when reused, whatever its worst grade (SPEC §5.5); (iii) `[p]^{n} ≐ □` is withdrawn: n·[p] is a finite log whose *instance* is the void pile ([[place.prime-power]], `([o^{p}]^{n}) ≃ void`), the pole is the limit end of the count of `[p]`, beyond every depth ([[place.two-ends]] restated), and only the exact void has the pole for its log; (iv) the engine's reading keeps every count finite — no residue is snapped to the pole, exponents do not wrap, the log of an exact number is read at full precision — the working precision is the depth plus a margin of as many shells again, a value that has lost more cannot be judged at the depth (SPEC §4.4), and the referee's depth-1…3 runs find no trunk law that holds at the working precision and fails at a smaller depth. *Offered with it:* the same measurement at the square is sound for a unit step under a square at ∞ and would upgrade some trunk imports; it is not applied there, pending Lui.

(h) **The generator of the torsion is a convention of the reading.** J_p is the log of the Teichmüller lift of the *least primitive root* mod p (the table in `tools/place.py`: 2 at 3, 5, 11, 13; 3 at 7, 17; …), and −1 at p = 2 — as 2πi rather than −2πi is the convention at ∞. The referee showed that under another generator the root form reads √4 as −2 at 5, 7 and 13 and i at 5 as τ(3) rather than τ(2): the *principal root* is canonical only on the principal units (where it is the principal unit with that power), and a cell that depends on the choice elsewhere says so ([[alg.sqrt4]]: `fails` at 3 is canonical — 4 is a principal unit there; at 5, 7, 13 it reads 2 by the table). Schema ranges may be given as tuples, `(p,n)=(2,2),(3,1),…`, where the depths differ by place (SPEC §2.7); the statement-table schemas of the first draft are gone (c).

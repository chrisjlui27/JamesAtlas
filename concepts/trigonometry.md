name: trigonometry
---
Cosine and sine are *defined* from the phase — half the sum, and half the difference over i, of a turn and its reverse — and Euler's formula is then the theorem that the two halves reassemble the turn. No lengths, no triangles: the angle is a count of J, in half-turns. The Pythagorean identity and the angle-addition formulas are owed.

name: phase
---
What J = `[<o>]` carries: a half-turn, read as iπ. Under an instance a phase is a sign (`(A J) ≐ <(A)>`), two of them are nothing (`(A J J) ≐ (A)`), and on the bare page they are kept exactly — which is the whole content of the seam decree. Fractions of J are the roots of unity; i is half of J.

Not a new kind of object: the forms could write every point of ℂ the chart reaches from rung `mult` on, and the ℂ reading has been the model since then. Rung C only *names* them — rectangular as a pile beside a count of i, polar as a log beside a count of J — and the two spellings of one number are not reduced to each other by any law.

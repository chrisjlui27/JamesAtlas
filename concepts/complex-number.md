name: complex-number
---
Not a new kind of object: the forms could write every point of ℂ the chart reaches from rung `mult` on, and the ℂ reading has been the model since then. Rung C only *names* them — rectangular as a pile beside a count of i, polar as a log beside a count of J — and the two spellings of one number are related only by Euler's formula: for a pure turn the bridge is a theorem (`C.euler-formula`, `C.modulus-of-polar`), while no law reduces a general form to either spelling.

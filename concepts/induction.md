The first gap. An equational calculus has no "for every pile"; the plan is a meta-move — the finite-set reading, where induction is the universal property of the free monoid on one point. Since rung `schema` the gap is *priced* rather than only named: a schema states a law for every n as a template, the engine checks it at every n in a declared range and checks that the hypothesis row is the law at a smaller n, and the passage to all n — the step no engine check reaches — is the gap, cited in that row and carried in the price.
